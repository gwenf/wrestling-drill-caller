How to use

Basics

  • Tap Start. After a 3-second count-in, moves are called out at random intervals during each round.
  • Pause freezes the round clock. Stop ends the session.
  • Your screen stays on while it runs, if your browser allows it.

Moves & tally

  • Tick the moves you want called. Add your own with the box under the list.
  • Every call counts as a rep. Green is this session, white is all time.
  • Use − / + to fix a rep you missed or didn't do.

Combos

  • A combo is 2–3 moves called in order, with a short pause after each one finishes.
  • The slider sets how often a call is a combo instead of a single move.
  • Build combos with the dropdowns. Each step counts toward that move's tally.

Timing

  • Seconds between calls: each gap is random within this range.
  • Shots vs. movement: slide right for more shots, left for more footwork and defense.

Saving your data

  • Everything is saved in this browser on this device. It isn't synced anywhere.
  • Clearing website data or history deletes it. On iPhone, Safari can delete it after 7 days unused unless the app is added to your Home Screen.
  • Use Backup → Download now and then. Upload restores it on any device.

No sound?

  • Turn off silent mode and turn the volume up. iPhone silent mode can mute browser speech.
  • Tap Start or Test first. Phones block audio until you tap the page.
  • Some in-app browsers (like Arc Search) don't support speech. Open the link in Safari or Chrome instead.
  • Bluetooth headphones: check they're connected before you tap Start.
  • Try another voice in Voice. On iPhone, download an Enhanced voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
  • No microphone access is needed. The app only speaks.

Install as an app

  • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.
  • Once installed it works offline.
